Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

PassGen does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Ever.

1. Overview

PassGen ("the app") is a local password generator for Android. It requires no account, no login, and no internet connection. All operations are performed entirely on your device.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information
  • No usage statistics or analytics
  • No crash reports sent to any server
  • No device identifiers
  • No passwords or clipboard contents

3. Internet Access

PassGen does not request internet permission and does not communicate with any server, third party, or external service.

4. Clipboard

When you tap "Copy", the generated password is placed in your device's clipboard using the standard Android API. This data never leaves your device through the app.

5. Third-Party Services

PassGen does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from any user, including children under the age of 13.
